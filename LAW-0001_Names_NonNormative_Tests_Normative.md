LAW-0001

Title: Names are Non-Normative; Tests are Normative

Primitive:

Names are non-normative.
Tests are normative.

Corollaries:

1. Category labels carry zero authority weight.
2. Architectural claims require conformance receipts.
3. A passing conformance test outranks any descriptive narrative.
4. A failing conformance test invalidates any descriptive claim.
5. No invariant may be defended by intent, explanation, or category identity.
6. Only reproducible, hash-verifiable test outcomes constitute authority.
